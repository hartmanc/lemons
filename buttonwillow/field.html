Stints and stops, top 20

Each bar is a run between pit stops, and each gap is a stop. The timing only shows when a car was in the pit lane, not who was driving, so a stop could be a driver change, fuel, a black flag or a repair. Our two known black flags are drawn as ticks, not gaps. Hover a bar for details.

Top 20: pace, stops and stints

Clean avg: mean of green laps that aren't in or out of the pits, the start lap, the first lap of day 2, or more than 25% over the car's median. Top 10%: mean of the car's fastest tenth of clean laps, which shows how fast the car can go. Stop cost: in-lap plus out-lap minus two clean laps, which is the time a stop really costs. Drive-throughs (under 45 s) aren't counted as stops. Grid: how long after the green (the leader's first crossing) this car first crossed the line, which is time driven but not counted as a lap.

Laps are laps driven; pen is laps taken off in the official results (penalty laps).
